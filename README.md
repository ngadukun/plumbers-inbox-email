One cold email of exactly 120 words, subject line included, from a bookkeeping service to a busy plumber who runs a two-van firm and reads email on a phone between jobs. It opens on his real week — early starts, emergency call-outs, invoices on the kitchen table at night — makes one small, concrete offer (£140 a month, no contract, last month's invoices tidied free as the first step), and ends with a single easy ask.

## The entry

> Subject: Your invoices, done by Friday
>
> Hi,
>
> If your day starts at six, jumps from a burst pipe to a boiler call-out, and ends with invoices on the kitchen table at ten, I can take that last part off you.
>
> I do the books for small plumbing firms. Send me your invoices, receipts and bank feed once a week. I chase late payers, sort your VAT, and send you one short list every Friday: who owes you, what you owe, and what is left.
>
> It costs £140 a month, no contract. First step is smaller: send me last month's invoices and I will tidy them free, so you can judge the work.
>
> Worth a quick try?
>
> Tom Ellis, ClearLedger Books

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This prints the email and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
